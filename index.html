SELL YOUR GEAR

You set the price. You describe the gear. You stay in control.

Paws It On is being built as a marketplace where pet parents can list their own used items with real photos, an honest condition, price, and delivery details.

  1. Photograph it. Show the actual item.
  2. Describe it. Be clear about wear and condition.
  3. Price it. Decide what it’s worth to you.
  4. Paws it on. Another pet parent gets what they need.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple for buyers. Simple for sellers.

If you’re buying

1

Find used gear. Browse secondhand items by category and condition.

2

Check the listing. See actual photos, seller details and delivery options.

3

Pay through the marketplace. Keep the transaction connected to the order record.

If you’re selling

1

Create a listing. Upload real photos and describe the item honestly.

2

Set your price. You decide what your used gear is worth.

3

Make a sale. Paws It On earns a clearly disclosed marketplace fee when an item sells.

ABOUT PAWS IT ON

Built around a very ordinary pet-owner problem.

Pets grow. Their needs change. And pet owners wind up with perfectly good crates, harnesses, gates, beds, carriers, toys and clothing that no longer fit their lives.

Paws It On is about connecting that unused gear with another pet parent who can use it—at a fairer price and with less waste.
